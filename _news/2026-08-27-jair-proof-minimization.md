title: Our paper is published in JAIR
---

Our paper “How to Discover Short, Shorter, and the Shortest Proofs of Unsatisfiability,” co-authored with [Koos van der Linden](https://kjgm.github.io/), [Gonçalo Homem de Almeida Correia](https://www.tudelft.nl/staff/g.correia/), [Mathijs de Weerdt](https://www.tudelft.nl/staff/m.m.deweerdt/), and [Emir Demirović](https://emirdemirovic.com), has been published in the [Journal of Artificial Intelligence Research](https://doi.org/10.1613/jair.1.22128)! You may also be interested to read [an accompanying post]({{ '/blog/2026/shortest-proofs/' | relative_url }}) for a more accessible overview of the ideas we introduce.
